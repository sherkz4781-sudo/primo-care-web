Cancellation & Reschedule Policy

Cancellations or reschedule requests made less than 48 hours before your scheduled time are subject to a fee of 20% of the Total Contract Price (TCP). Requests made 48 hours or more in advance are free of charge. Approved reschedules take effect immediately and replace your prior scheduled time. Primo Care may waive fees at its discretion.
